About Explore Vietnam

Explore Vietnam is a student website created to introduce some of the country's wonderful travel destinations.

Our goal is to inspire curiosity about Vietnam's landscapes, culture, and history through simple stories and photography.

This project also demonstrates accessible HTML and modern CSS techniques, including Flexbox, Grid, hover effects, and responsive layouts.
